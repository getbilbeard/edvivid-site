# EdVivid School Update Intake Form

Create this in Google Forms after a school agrees to work with EdVivid. Link responses to a Google Sheet and create one shared Google Drive delivery folder per school.

## Form title

EdVivid School Update Submission

## Form description

Send your upcoming school updates in one place. Include only information approved for public communication. Do not submit student records, private contact information, or unapproved student images.

## Questions

1. School or district name
   - Short answer, required

2. Primary contact name
   - Short answer, required

3. Primary contact email
   - Short answer, required, response validation: email

4. What should we create?
   - Multiple choice, required
   - Morning news segment
   - Event announcement video and flyer
   - Both

5. Requested delivery date
   - Date, required

6. Where will this be shared?
   - Checkboxes
   - Morning announcements or classroom screens
   - Parent or family messages
   - School website or social media
   - Other

7. List the updates or events to include
   - Paragraph, required
   - Helper text: For each item include event name, date, time, location, audience, action needed, and any link or deadline.

8. Required wording, links, or calls to action
   - Paragraph, optional

9. School colors, mascot, or branding notes
   - Paragraph, optional

10. Google Drive link to approved photos, logos, flyers, or source materials
    - Short answer, optional
    - Helper text: Make sure the folder allows EdVivid to view the files.

11. Approval confirmation
    - Checkbox, required
    - I confirm that these details are accurate and that my school has permission to use the submitted materials.

## Internal workflow

1. School submits the form.
2. EdVivid reviews the intake and confirms the scope and delivery date.
3. EdVivid sends an invoice or accepts the school's purchase order.
4. EdVivid produces the video, flyer, and caption.
5. School approves the draft.
6. EdVivid delivers final files to the school's shared Google Drive folder.
