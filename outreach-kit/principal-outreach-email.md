# EdVivid Principal Outreach Email

Use this as an individual email or mail merge. Replace every bracketed field before sending. Do not send it as one BCC blast.

- A faster way to share [School Name] announcements
- Could this save [School Name] staff time?
- A simpler way to share [School Name]'s news
- Could this help [School Name]?
