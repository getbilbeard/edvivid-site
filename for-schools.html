Questions, answered

Simple for your staff.

How do we start?+

Request a sample or send an inquiry. EdVivid will confirm your needs and send a proposal for the right service level.

How does payment work?+

After the scope is confirmed, your school receives an invoice. Schools can use their normal purchasing process, including a purchase order when needed. Zelle is available for approved small partnerships when school policy permits.

Do we need a new account or platform?+

No. EdVivid works with your existing communication channels. Finished files are organized in a shared Google Drive folder.

Can we use our own school mascot and branding?+

Yes. Your school identity, mascot, colors, and approved materials shape the finished news and announcement content.
